# What to Track

## User Profile
- Name, role, location
- Technical level (beginner / intermediate / expert)
- Primary language and communication style
- Preferred tools and stack

## Preferences
- Code style preferences (tabs vs spaces, naming conventions)
- Response length preference (concise vs detailed)
- Preferred frameworks and libraries
- Tools they dislike or avoid

## Ongoing Work
- Current projects and their goals
- Active problems being solved
- Deadlines and priorities
- Team members and their roles

## Decisions Made
- Architecture decisions and their rationale
- Tools chosen and why
- Things explicitly rejected and why

## Working Style
- When they work (timezone, hours)
- How they prefer to receive feedback
- What frustrates them
- What they find helpful

## Do NOT Track
- Passwords or credentials
- Financial account details
- Health information unless explicitly shared for a relevant reason
- Anything the user hasn't explicitly shared
