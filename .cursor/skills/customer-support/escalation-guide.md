# Escalation Guide

## Escalate Immediately (P0)
- Data loss or corruption
- Security breach or suspected unauthorized access
- Legal threats or regulatory complaints
- Service down for paying customers
- Executive or VIP contacts

## Escalate Within 1 Hour (P1)
- Issue affecting multiple customers simultaneously
- Payment processing failures
- Customer threatening public escalation (social media, press)
- Bug causing data inconsistency

## Escalate Within 24 Hours (P2)
- Repeated issue not resolved after 2 attempts
- Customer requested manager specifically
- Complex technical issue outside support scope
- Accessibility or compliance concern

## Escalation Template
```
ESCALATION — [P0/P1/P2]
Customer: [name/email]
Issue: [1-sentence description]
Impact: [what is affected]
Steps taken: [what was already tried]
Customer sentiment: [calm / frustrated / angry]
Recommended action: [what you think should happen]
```

## Do NOT Escalate
- General how-to questions (answer them yourself)
- Feature requests (log in tracker)
- Complaints about pricing (handle with empathy + offer alternatives)
