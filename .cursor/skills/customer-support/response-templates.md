# Response Templates

## Bug Report — Acknowledged
> Thank you for reporting this. I've confirmed the issue on our end and have flagged it to our engineering team. I'll update you as soon as we have a fix — typically within [timeframe]. In the meantime, [workaround if available].

## Feature Not Available
> That's a great idea! This feature isn't available yet, but I've added your request to our feedback tracker. I'll let you know if it's added to our roadmap. In the meantime, [alternative approach].

## Billing Issue — Refund
> I completely understand your frustration. I've reviewed your account and processed a refund of [amount]. It should appear on your statement within 3-5 business days. I've also [corrected the underlying issue].

## Account Access Problem
> Let's get you back in. Please try [steps]. If that doesn't work, reply to this message and I'll manually reset your access within 1 hour.

## "I Want to Cancel"
> I'm sorry to hear that. Before I process the cancellation, I'd like to understand what wasn't working for you — your feedback helps us improve. If there's anything specific that led to this decision, I may be able to help resolve it. If you'd still like to cancel, I'll take care of it right away.

## Escalation to Manager
> I want to make sure this gets the attention it deserves. I'm escalating your case to our senior support team, who will reach out within [timeframe]. Your case number is [#].
